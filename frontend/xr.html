In the headset

  1. Pin a place: point at the menu item Pin Bench 3, pull the trigger (or pinch), then point at the real bench and pull again. It stays there next time.
  2. Read the room: a label floats over each pinned place with what ORBIT believes is there and how sure it is. It updates live.
  3. Ask: point at a label to hear it, or hold the grip button (or choose Talk to Orbi) and speak.
  4. Approve: when Orbi prepares an action, say “yes” or choose Yes, authorize. You still do the action yourself.
